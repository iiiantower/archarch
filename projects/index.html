Oakwood Resiclence

Bringing together the intimacy of home and the quiet attentiveness of hotel living, each residence is shaped through proportion, materiality, and natural light. The result is a calm, understated environment where light, texture, and order quietly enrich the everyday.

Comfort is found in thoughtful details. Service remains discreet, ever present but never intrusive. Every gesture is intentional, allowing each stay to feel effortless and instinctively lived in.

Oakwood Font

Oakwood Font is a lightweight sans-serif type system shaped to bring a quieter presence to the visual environment.

Guided by proportion, curvature, and the body's natural perception of form, each letter is drawn to support an effortless reading rhythm. Subtle openings at key junctions—most notably in characters such as b, d, and e—soften structural precision, allowing the typeface to breathe more naturally within its surroundings.

The result is a typographic voice that feels calm, understated, and quietly integrated into the spatial experience.

Feihua Youling

Food Brand Identity Design

Founded by a wildlife conservationist, Feihua Youling is a food brand exploring the distinctive landscapes and rare produce of the regions surrounding China's national parks and nature reserves. We have worked with the brand from the beginning, developing its strategy, visual identity, and packaging for its first honey collection, translating its philosophy into a tangible experience.

The photographs were taken by park rangers during their daily work, capturing nature as they see it, without staging or embellishment. Rather than creating a fixed visual hierarchy, we placed photographs, text, and details of origin side by side, giving each equal importance. The design remains simple and unobtrusive, allowing the real landscapes, people, and wildlife behind the products to tell their own stories.

Across material, structure, and scale, we sought a sense of lightness and simplicity. Recycled paper, a lightweight box, and minimal material use reflect a simple intention: to use less and waste less. Rather than creating something to keep, we wanted to create something to feel — a lasting connection to the land, its life, and the people behind it.

Editorial

Inspired by plants, animals, and people, we created a series of brush-painted symbols exploring the relationship between nature and conservation. Rooted in the forms and traces of the local landscape, these expressive marks become a visual language beyond words, connecting people to the natural world through a shared sense of life and care.

Packaging

Through local ingredients and the stories of their origins, the brand seeks to foster a genuine and meaningful connection between people and the land. The packaging for its first honey collection carries this philosophy forward, allowing the land, time, and emotions behind each product to come into view.

A Letter from the Wild — The story begins with a handwritten letter from a ranger at Giant Panda National Park, offering a personal introduction to the land where the honey originates. From there, photographs taken by the rangers reveal the landscape as they see it in their daily work. These images, together with stories of the land, its produce, and its people, come together in a booklet.

Honey is a natural record of time, slowly gathered and formed through changing seasons and cycles of bloom. We wanted the packaging to unfold like a story, revealing the quiet poetry of nature with each layer. From the pleasure of giving and receiving to the simple rituals of unwrapping, exploring, and tasting, every moment invites a closer connection to the land. What begins as a fleeting experience unfolds into a lasting bond with the brand.

Rangers’ Hat

The Poetics of Nature — The name Feihua Youling takes inspiration from Feihua Ling, a traditional Chinese poetry game once played by scholars and poets, where flowers inspire a playful exchange of poetry, celebrating nature and human sentiment. Honey, the core product of the brand, originate from Flowers, the nature’s language, shape of time. Through flowers, we find poetry in the changing seasons and the rhythms of nature, revealing a quiet connection between people and the natural world.

We wanted to preserve this sense of wonder, drawing attention to the small sigh of life that emerge slowly over time but often go unnoticed. By allowing design to take a step back, we move from RARE to CARE — beyond the value of rare things towards a way of life shaped by appreciation for nature.

The visual identity began with the idea of a breath that brings us back to nature. We translated “the rhythm of breathing” into a visual language: inhaling, as forms rise and unfold like flowers in bloom; a moment of stillness, leaving space for anticipation; and exhaling, as everything gently settles into place. Wild yet ordered. Free yet considered. A balance shaped by nature and our understanding of it. It also speaks to our ability to slow down, find clarity, and reconnect with our surroundings. The letterforms expand and contract in a gentle rhythm, balancing organic movement with a considered sense of structure and offering a visual expression of nature's own pace.
